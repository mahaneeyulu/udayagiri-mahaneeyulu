About this book
Udayagiri Mahaneeyulu delves into the rich and layered history of Udayagiri — one of Andhra Pradesh's most historically significant towns. The book brings together accounts of the great rulers who governed its lands, the historians who documented its past, and the extraordinary leaders and personalities who left an indelible mark on the region.

Through meticulous research and detailed narration, the author Mohammed Haneef Khalidi presents a tribute to the grandeur of Udayagiri's heritage, making this an essential read for anyone who wishes to understand the depth of its history and the greatness of its people.
